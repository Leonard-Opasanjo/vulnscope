Method and limits

VulnScope performs passive checks only: it fetches the home page and a short list of well-known paths with GET, sends one OPTIONS and one TRACE request, one request with a probe Origin header, and inspects the TLS handshake and certificate. It does not log in, submit forms, fuzz parameters or exploit anything. The score deducts {{ 40 }} points per critical, 20 per high, 10 per medium and 4 per low finding from 100; grades are A (90+), B (75+), C (60+), D (40+) and F. A good grade means the common configuration mistakes are absent; it is not a penetration test and says nothing about application logic, authentication or injection flaws.
